Introduction

This is a workshop on Python Bytecode.

The format will be:

  • Some explanatory introductory information (15 minutes per section) followed by...
  • Some exercises (5 minutes) and ...
  • Discussion (optional)

Ater this workshop, hopefully you'll have a deeper appreciation of Python bytecode, and you may understand bytecode better with respect to reverse engineering.

Welcome to Bsides NYC 2025! This is my first time attending. And it is also the first workshop that I have given on this topic.

There is probably too much material and there is a lot more that I could have prepared. So we probably will not get to all of it. But don't feel that you have to do all of the exercises. Just do what you can.

The workshop materials will be available; you can come back to this afterwards. I will be around all day.

And if you find what I have to say too slow, you can skip ahead and do exercises. Please use Slack to ask questions; Please help out others if you can.
