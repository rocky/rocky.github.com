In some of the examples I selected lesser-used Python implementations. In particular, I used PyPy 3.9 in example6. pycdc does not support this kind of bytecode. Pylingual may report errors, because it is confusing CPython with PyPy, which are largely the same. For this conference, I hacked the latest iteration of the uncompyle6/decompyle3 decompilers to make it work. But personally, I think it would be pretty easy to extend pylingual.io to handle PyPy properly.
